# Nimbus Notes: Help Center FAQ

## Plans and pricing

Nimbus Notes has three plans. The Free plan includes up to 3 notebooks, 100 MB of storage and sync across 2 devices. The Pro plan costs $6 per month or $60 per year and includes unlimited notebooks, 20 GB of storage, unlimited devices and version history for 30 days. The Team plan costs $10 per user per month (billed annually) and adds shared workspaces, admin controls, SSO and 1 year of version history.

Students and teachers with a valid school email get 50% off Pro. Nonprofits can request a free Team workspace for up to 10 users by contacting support.

## Refunds and cancellation

You can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period.

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your first payment on any paid plan. After 14 days, monthly plans are not refundable. Annual plans cancelled after 14 days receive a prorated refund for unused full months.

## Downgrading

If you downgrade from Pro to Free, none of your notes are deleted. Notebooks beyond the Free limit of 3 become read-only until you upgrade again or reduce your notebook count. Attachments over the 100 MB storage limit remain viewable but you cannot upload new files.

## Attachments

Free accounts can attach files up to 10 MB each. Pro and Team accounts can attach files up to 250 MB each. Supported previews include PDF, images, audio and Office documents.

## Exporting your data

Go to Settings > Account > Export data. You can export everything as Markdown (a ZIP with one folder per notebook), as HTML, or as a single JSON file. Exports of large accounts are prepared in the background and emailed to you as a download link valid for 7 days.

## Deleted notes and notebooks

Deleted notes and notebooks go to Trash and can be restored for 30 days. Open the Trash from the sidebar, select the item and choose Restore. Pro and Team users can also restore earlier versions of a note from the version history menu.
